• Implementing image-to-text translation posed significant challenges as the system
grappled with accurately identifying text, often leading to errors in information
extraction and translation. Despite efforts to refine the process, issues persisted,
impacting the system's ability to reliably interpret and translate text from images.
3. Dialog Management:
• It was not easy managing dialogue flow to keep context and give meaningful replies
especially when dealing with multiple user queries concurrently. (Tom Bocklisch,
2017) We encountered difficulties with the bot interpreting the query as per the user's
request, resulting in incorrect or confusing information.
